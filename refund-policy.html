Refund Policy

At Surendra App Development Solutions, we strive to provide high-quality app development, web development, and software solutions. This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds may be granted for our services.

By engaging our services, you agree to the terms of this Refund Policy.

1. Service-Based Projects

Our services include custom mobile app development, web development, UI/UX design, software development, maintenance, and related digital solutions.

Since our services involve significant time, expertise, planning, and resource allocation, payments made for completed work and delivered milestones are generally non-refundable.

2. Project Cancellation

Clients may request project cancellation before development work begins.

  • If cancellation is requested before project initiation, a partial refund may be considered after deducting consultation, planning, and administrative charges.
  • If development work has already started, refunds will be evaluated based on the completed work and resources utilized.
  • No refund will be issued for completed project phases or approved milestones.

3. Milestone Payments

For projects executed in milestones, payments made for completed milestones are non-refundable once the deliverables have been shared, approved, or accepted by the client.

4. Change of Mind

Refund requests based on a change of mind, business decisions, market conditions, or preference changes after project commencement will not qualify for a refund.

5. Delays Caused by Client

We are not responsible for project delays resulting from the client's failure to provide content, approvals, feedback, credentials, or other required information.

Such delays do not qualify for refunds.

6. Exceptional Refund Requests

In exceptional circumstances, refund requests may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis at our sole discretion.

Approval of any refund request is not guaranteed and will depend on the project status, work completed, and contractual obligations.

7. Processing of Approved Refunds

If a refund is approved, it will be processed through the original payment method whenever possible.

Refund processing may take 7-15 business days depending on the payment provider and banking institution.
